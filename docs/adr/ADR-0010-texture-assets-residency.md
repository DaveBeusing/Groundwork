# ADR-0010 — Deterministic texture assets, asynchronous upload and GPU residency

**Status:** Proposed for architecture review; not accepted or implemented by this document
**Date:** 2026-11-15
**Decision owner:** Asset/Rendering contracts, D3D12 GPU lifetime, diagnostics; WYU asset-content integration

## Problem and observed baseline

The renderer already provides bounded terrain and prop upload admission, resource retirement and diagnostic hooks. It does not establish a general-purpose cooked texture asset pipeline, mip streaming, texture-residency service or stable material texture binding in the inspected repository tree. Texture work must coexist with protected collision/edit publication and the current exclusive D3D12 owner.

## Proposed decision


1. **Cook immutable assets offline.** TEX-01 owns a versioned manifest and deterministic portable importer. The portable asset ID is `sha256:` followed by 64 lowercase hex digits. Hash a canonical, versioned serialization of the source digest, importer and recipe versions, format, dimensions, mip layout, color space and exact cooked payload bytes. Document the serialization, ordering and toolchain. Paths and timestamps do not enter the digest. Record source identity separately. Require identical bytes and digest across clean runs with the same inputs and toolchain.
2. **Validate the texture envelope.** Start with 2D single-layer RGBA8 and BC1 cooked fixtures, including linear and sRGB interpretations. Importing validated precompressed blocks is sufficient for the BC1 fixture. Defer other formats and arrays until supported with tests. Validate dimensions, full declared mip layout, format, offsets, lengths, overflow and color metadata before allocating the payload. The importer accepts finite caps for source, decoded/scratch and cooked bytes plus dimensions and mips. Deny input that cannot fit before the corresponding allocation. Use semantic mip filtering, including linear-light color and documented normal-map behavior. Record unsupported, truncated and corrupt inputs as controlled failures.
3. **Separate content identity from runtime bindings.** A palette references an immutable cooked ID and expected map semantic. The D3D12 owner resolves it to a device/renderer-local resource and descriptor generation. Recheck request, asset, palette, affected slot/group revision, world/view, terrain and epoch at completion and again at the render-owned frame cut in ADR-0009. Validate each change against its target, then compose a new immutable full set. Unrelated slot changes do not stale a matching request. Preserve frame-latched versions through last use. Stale or superseded work cannot publish even on unchanged terrain. Immutable validated payloads can be reused independently of old frame bindings.
4. **Reserve asynchronous resources before dispatch.** At runtime read, validate and unpack cooked data off the render and simulation threads. Coalesce duplicate demand. Atomically reserve a bounded visual resource vector before dispatch or payload allocation, including request, worker, result/handoff slots, input, scratch and completed payload bytes, copy-footprint staging, aligned native allocation and descriptor/heap capacity. Include resident predecessor, candidate and retiring generations and their old/new overlap in admission. A reservation may settle or transfer to the next owner without an uncharged gap. Rejection and saturation are nonblocking with bounded retry. Do not wait for a downstream slot while holding an unreserved result. Cancellation or supersession prevents publication immediately and keeps worker and payload charges until proven drain and handoff settlement. Keep native charges through eligible last-use retirement or confirmed removal.
5. **Share live state and direct-queue custody.** TEX-02 follows RPA-02 and uses its exclusive owner handoff for native state, descriptors and custody. A copy and required subresource transitions use the same direct queue, with only initialized mips exposed. Install the new immutable binding after its assigned copy fence retires normally at an authorized frame boundary. Wake the owner once for an actionable result handoff and observe outstanding copy retirement through a bounded nonblocking wake or poll independent of new frames. Request one redraw when the binding becomes eligible in a still-authorized drawable view, including while paused. Protected first content always has priority. Separate staging credit return after the last copy from descriptor/resource retirement after all copy/draw uses. Retain exact descriptor versions, their heap and backing resources. Failed or unassigned signals, fence sentinel, resize, stop and device removal follow ADR-0008 and the existing owner policy.
6. **Protect both capacity and queue progress.** Keep disjoint visual CPU and GPU credits and preserve WP11 movement, first-edit and recovery envelopes plus WP16's separate 2 MiB prop upload cap. At the existing render-owner arbitration point, service eligible protected staging and presentation before admitting optional copies. Cap per-turn CPU work, each non-preemptible copy quantum and the aggregate submitted, unretired visual copy work ahead of protected work across textures and props. Recheck eligibility before another quantum. Split or reject work larger than an admissible slice. Ready protected frames use the current valid binding or fallback without waiting for a texture; no extra terrain brick requests. Qualify healthy-queue delay with a protected request arriving immediately after the largest admitted visual submission. Qualify a stuck fence separately using the existing bounded hold/stop and retained custody. Adopt reference ceiling values with #88 while preserving all existing caps.
7. **Reclaim real backing in TEX-03.** Distinguish requested and sampleable mips, uploaded bytes and charged backing allocation. A resident binding exposes at least one initialized usable mip. Use separately sized committed mip tiers and whole-resource replacement or eviction to a pre-reserved fallback. Preflight the actual native descriptor and cooked-to-native mip mapping before reservation or allocation, including TEX-02 static level zero and each TEX-03 tier. For BC1, validate each tier's top-level dimensions against the adapter's unaligned-block support. When a tier is invalid, deterministically choose an admissible legal tier or the existing fallback. Exposing fewer mips in an unchanged allocation releases no native credit. Pre-charge the full replacement overlap and staging. If it does not fit, retain an admissible tier or use an allocated fallback and wait for retirement before allocating again. Release backing credit only after the old allocation actually releases. Prioritize first usable mips ahead of refinements with deterministic age/service bounds and stable tie-breaks across eligible persistent requests. Apply that service bound across shared visual arbitration when compatible spare turns recur with both props and textures ready. Establish eligibility before assigning the visual turn. Maintain protected-work priority and the existing WP16 prop byte cap. Use hysteresis for refinement. Under hard exhaustion use the fallback and a finite retry policy. Tiled resources and GPU compaction require a separate measured decision.
8. **Separate visual fallback from terminal custody faults.** Missing, damaged, stale or oversized data and quota denial select a deterministic visible fallback or still-eligible lower tier and bounded diagnostics. Uncertain native submission closes affected admission and uses the existing renderer/runtime terminal stop path. Retain custody and separately report first fault, signal assignment, confirmed device removal and incomplete cleanup independently of a disabled, saturated or failing optional sink. Confirmed removal can settle custody. Any later renderer must use a fresh healthy epoch with existing recovery policy.

## Alternatives

- **Load full-resolution images synchronously each time:** rejected; frame stalls and unbounded allocation risk.
- **Keep all textures fully resident:** useful for tiny fixtures, not a general large-world policy.
- **Implement virtual texturing first:** deferred pending measured demand and DX12 sparse-resource complexity.
- **Immutable cooked assets + bounded mip residency (selected proposal):** reduces hot-path uncertainty and supports incremental quality.

## Implementation slices

- TEX-01: portable descriptor/validation and deterministic offline cook test fixture; no D3D12 requirement.
- TEX-02: one bounded D3D12 static upload/descriptor/fallback path on the RPA-02 handoff with WARP readback and failure tests. A fully allocated static mip chain is charged in full. Dynamic promotion and demotion start in TEX-03.
- TEX-03: budgeted asynchronous mip promotion/demotion, eviction, residency diagnostics and backpressure under simultaneous WP11/WP16 traffic.
- INT-01: one WYU-independent reference terrain material and image/performance regression; WYU-specific recipes/content integrate in their own PR against an exact verified package set.

## Required evidence / acceptance


- TEX-01 proves clean-run byte identity and validates sRGB/linear data, mip dimensions and layout, BC block sizing and malformed, mismatched or oversized inputs at each configured limit. Reject overflow without unbounded allocation. Record tooling and fixture license/provenance.
- TEX-02 uses positive RGBA8/BC1 WARP sampling tests and injects stale palette/binding/device completion, cancellation or saturation at queued, running, handoff, recorded and submitted stages. Hold the result consumer and existing draw/copy fences to verify exact-once credit settlement and protected progress. Exercise reversed independent-slot completion, same-slot supersession and replacement of a paused visible fallback through a delayed copy fence without camera or tick events. Check pre-execution abort, post-execution failed signal, fake sentinel, resize, stop timeout and later confirmed removal with diagnostics off, full or throwing. Verify correct fallback pixels and no clean-shutdown report while custody is unresolved.
- TEX-03 tests sustained promotion traffic, stable first usable-mip service on admitted routes, and finite retry under exhaustion. Hold the predecessor draw while saturating texture, terrain and prop budgets. Verify no backing credit on logical demotion, reserved overlap at the small native cap, and actual allocated bytes falling after permitted release. Record texture/prop/terrain counters separately. Sustain prop and refinement traffic with recurring compatible visual turns. Under a forced no-unaligned-block capability, upload a valid BC1 chain whose proposed smaller tier has an invalid top mip. Verify legal-tier or fallback pixels, no invalid native creation, exact credit settlement and custody of a held predecessor draw. INT-01 checks combined shadow/material heaps and held descriptor versions on WARP and NVIDIA with Debug Layer/DRED.
- Measure upload and allocation bytes, descriptor slots, native high-water, CPU/GPU p50/p95/p99, queue waits and protected publication latency on the same #88 reference workload. Use #89 for fault qualification. Preserve immutable package provenance and locked WYU restores.

## Consequences / approval

Requires importer and asset-validation work, stable GPU descriptor ownership and observable memory budgets. Enables textured PBR, HDR and larger visual environments without making world state dependent on graphics residency. Four independent ADR-0007 specialist reviews, sequential integration review and explicit owner sign-off are required before accepting the decision or merging implementation.
