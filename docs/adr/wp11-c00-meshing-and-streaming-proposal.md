# WP11-C00 meshing and streaming decision proposal

Status: proposed for architecture review in [draft PR #77](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/pull/77). This addendum records decision evidence. The normative gate sequence is in [C00-4](../architecture/wp11-procedural-planet-contract.md#c00-4--mesher-selection-and-qualification-gates-r04r05r09). The owner closed the C00 measurement campaign on 2026-11-10. The architecture gate still requires review and explicit acceptance of the resulting head.

## Meshing and collision

Propose Marching Cubes with Transvoxel transitions as the B01 implementation baseline behind a replaceable meshing interface. In the nine shared Workstation fixtures it had lower median extraction time and managed allocations than the tested Dual Contouring candidate. In the eight-region pressure fixture, p50/p95/p99 were 9.23/10.65/11.11 ms versus 28.73/41.33/42.69 ms. Dual Contouring remains a useful comparison for sharp features and thin structures. [Comparative evidence](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/issues/78#issuecomment-6727821654).

Render and collision derive from the same immutable density authority and may use separate bounded representations. Admission uses the full current C00-1C/C00-3 publication identity, including committed terrain/collision identity, renderer epoch, view generation, origin and the registered frame as applicable, so a matching density revision alone cannot admit a rebased view or stale ACK. A render-mesh hit or miss alone cannot certify coverage for thin features, edits or LOD seams. A01 must first qualify canonical chart identity. B01, C02 and Q02 must then qualify deterministic shared boundaries, winding and normals, material continuity, mixed-LOD corners and cube-chart edges, and collision parity against the density authority. Preserve local refinement and bounded query work with fail-closed admission when matching coverage is unavailable. The edited cuboid and up-to-eight-brush fixtures support the design but do not establish general planetary collision correctness. [Geometry and collision evidence](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/issues/78#issuecomment-6735512096).

## Capacity and latency

Use a fixed bounded meshing pool with a reserved protected worker and capped speculative vertex storage. Reserve CPU and GPU credits before allocation. Charge staged, leased, obsolete and retiring geometry until ownership ends and any required fence completes. Keep old coherent coverage while streaming is saturated, and preserve protected movement, first-edit and recovery paths under the contract's admission rules. [Saturated terrain route and resource evidence](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/issues/78#issuecomment-6737895324).

For the reproducible C00 Workstation fixture, propose a Frame Fence gate of nearest-rank p99 ≤ 11 ms and maximum ≤ 16 ms. Apply it to the aggregate and Recovery separately. The direct NVIDIA 60 Hz/HAGS/Limit-2/ALLOW_TEARING repeat used a full CPU queue and blocked GPU streaming. Across 14,400 frames the p99/max were 5.7139/6.6131 ms. The 4,800 Recovery frames measured 5.8125/6.1200 ms. Both populations had zero frames above 11 ms. This is a reference measurement for this fixture and presentation mode. The reference measurement follows the exact population and error rules in C00-4. Production presentation policy and qualification on the 8-core/32-GiB class remain Q01/Q02 gates. The C00 measurements also leave the 512 MiB CPU terrain / 256 MiB native geometry ceilings as planning guardrails pending production ownership and residency evidence. [Final repeat and trace configuration](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/issues/78#issuecomment-6778821188).

The earlier movement Present outliers involved NVIDIA work items queued by a WDF timer. The caller that armed the timer is unknown. Neither outlier recurred in the repeat. Keep the diagnosis open for recurrence that breaches the budget. The completed campaign schedules no further runs. [Scheduler and power evidence](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/issues/78#issuecomment-6778250835).
