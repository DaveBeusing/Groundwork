# WP16 portable prop cells

PropCellAdmission belongs to the renderer owner. Publish the canonical coverage cut before requesting cells. The producer supplies the canonical partition and submits requests in its chosen priority order. The admission object never requests terrain or starts workers. Request tokens are specific to a world, renderer epoch, terrain revision, coverage generation and request generation. Advance the coverage generation on every new coverage cut, including same-revision streaming changes.

Reserve a maximum instance count before dispatch. Stage sorted placements by ordinal and use CopyStaged to prepare an upload. Install only at a frame boundary after the provider admits the upload and rechecks the token. If capacity is unavailable, keep the old valid cell and retry or cancel. Publication changes, cancellation and eviction invalidate tokens immediately. Unstaged work keeps its worker slot and instance reservation until CompleteWorker acknowledges that the worker has stopped and released its terrain lease and result. Call it also for an undispatched request or when discarding a result after failed staging. Successful TryStage is the completion handoff after worker exit and lease release. It transfers the reservation to staged data until install or invalidation. Unaffected resident cells may survive an explicit terrain invalidation cut when their coverage remains valid. The native provider owns the quota and fence lifetime of copies that outlive portable eviction or replacement.

Pass six inward unit frustum planes relative to the canonical eye and caller-owned scratch sized to the resident count. Selection returns references sorted by model, material, LOD, cell and ordinal. LOD enters the near band at or below NearEnter and leaves above NearExit. A newly resident instance starts in the far state until its first selection. The caller groups contiguous equal draw keys.

The 32-byte, four-byte-aligned payload stores float3 translation at byte 0, quaternion XYZW at byte 12, float uniform scale at byte 20, RGBA8 tint at byte 24 and uint cell slot at byte 28. Each quaternion component occupies a signed 16-bit lane rounded to nearest even at scale 32767. XYZW begins at the least-significant lane. Normalize after decode. Per-component quantization is at most half a lane step before decode normalization, with a conservative rotation error below 0.01 degrees for valid input. Cell-local translation is bounded to +/-4096 m per axis. Subtract canonical origins in double precision before conversion to the GPU coordinate system.

This slice accounts for portable cell and instance counts. The D3D12 slice adds native upload and retirement accounting, draw submission and the Windows GPU qualification gates in the [WP16 contract](architecture/wp16-instancing-streaming-contract.md).
