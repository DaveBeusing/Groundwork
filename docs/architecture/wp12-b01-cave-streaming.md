# WP12-B01 · Cave streaming and terrain publication

## Runtime contract

- A V2 terrain host uses `VolumeStreamingSelection.SelectCave` for each protected publication. Supply the swept capsule and, for an edit, the canonical dependencies returned by `TryGetEditDependencies`. The selection fits the camera halo, the sweep and the edit footprint into the bounded brick window. Bricks touched by protected interest require stride one. An oversized or invalid request fails before staging. Ray and capsule queries against a cave publication fail closed when their bounds reach outside the plan or touch a coarse brick. Render-only coarse geometry may remain elsewhere in the window.
- Cave work uses the existing disjoint movement, edit, recovery and speculative reservations. View changes cancel stale work and hold movement. A renderer-bound edit waits for matching CPU geometry, native staging and a retired mask before a later CommitResults cut can publish. Movement reopens at a BeginTick after the matching Present acknowledgment. A retained old lease still refers to its original geometry and collision.
- Regular V2 triangles retain their Marching Cubes winding. Where a wider density-gradient stencil points across another cave wall at a sharp crease, the emitted triangle uses its outward face normal as a hard crease. Smooth normals on the same canonical vertex agree between output neighborhoods. The hard-crease exception is face-local and leaves positions and winding unchanged. Existing transition validation still promotes unsupported mixed layouts to stride one. Geometry and collision use the same triangle positions.
- A natural ore deposit uses the generator's canonical material-cell key. A removal consumes it only when the cell changes from at least one negative corner to all nonnegative corners. Partial removal does not grant a deposit. Placement records provenance even when its material ID matches the natural ore. Removing placed material cannot award natural yield. If a placed cell obscures a natural deposit and is then fully removed, mark the underlying deposit depleted without a reward. On later refill the depletion flag survives. With no placed override the natural material resolves to host rock.
- An ore-producing edit requires an immutable `IVolumeOreInventoryState` in the world root. The host advances its inventory scope revision on ordinary conflicting changes and stages its own yield through the inventory adapter. Groundwork checks the inventory target and revision at barrier entry and in the composed state. Eligible ordinary batches retain their dispositions. Their admitted read and command scopes and direct structural targets cannot overlap the inventory target. ECS uses admitted partition membership. Other scope policies without membership information conservatively treat a matching partition as a possible overlap. A conflict or rejected staging leaves geometry, depletion and inventory unchanged. The accepted revision, resulting state and edit receipt share the existing root swap. The adapter must be bounded and side-effect free because preparation can be retried.
- V2 uses the existing bounded overlay row storage. Material rows encode the UInt16 material in the low bits, depletion in bit 16 and placement provenance in bit 17. These rows count against the existing overlay quota and participate in the plan digest and checkpoint bytes. V1 rows retain their encoding. C01 owns the external checkpoint codec and NTFS recovery integration for V2.

## Verification

The deterministic portal fixture uses seed 42, radius 128 m and portal centre (64,33,26). A capsule crosses in both directions, and a query outside admitted fine coverage holds. A protected edit adds a barrier over the brick boundary with the old revision retained and the speculative queue reservation occupied. A camera rebase and the matching native publication preserve collision, depth and material agreement. The WARP readback is required in Windows CI. Desktop qualification enables the debug layer and DRED on WARP and NVIDIA. Reference capacity qualification and production acceptance remain open.
