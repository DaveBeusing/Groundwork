# WP15 · Sunlight and near-terrain shadow contract

WP15 adds optional directional sunlight and two near-terrain shadow cascades to Groundwork D3D12. WYU supplies the light from the same accepted simulation time and canonical sun as the [Package G sky](../package-g-handover.md). Package B supplies the biome and material colors. [Package E](../package-e-handover.md) supplies the local distance fog and establishes the frame, publication and handoff behavior. [Groundwork #113](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/issues/113) · [WYU #43](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/wyu/issues/43)

## Frame and mesh boundary

- Add optional, fixed-size lighting to terrain scene and paired volume-camera submissions while preserving existing constructors and deconstruction. Carry a unit canonical direction **toward** the sun, nonnegative linear RGB direct and ambient gains, and the view data needed to fit both cascades from that frame. Validate finite values, normalized direction and rigid camera basis, and bounded gains before incrementing sequence or modifying pending frame and mesh state. Fix and test numeric tolerances in the renderer PR. Clear-only and sky-only handoff masks use their existing paths.
- Reuse the mesher's normals with the existing vertex positions, indices and packed material colors. Pack each normal into at most four additional GPU bytes per resident near-terrain vertex. Record the encoding, maximum angular error and invalid-normal behavior, and test roundtrip and both sides of an LOD seam. Rebase translates positions and leaves canonical orientation and normals intact. Legacy callers that omit lighting retain the existing unlit color and fog output.
- In linear color, apply the existing vertex color and scene tint, multiply RGB by ambient gain plus direct gain times max(dot(normal, sun), 0) times shadow visibility, then apply Package E fog. Alpha and terrain depth stay on their original paths. Treat the host's scene tint as a material multiplier. WYU transfers E's daylight factor into the chosen WP15 gains when lighting is enabled so ambient is applied once. G remains a background pass. Orbit, other lights, and prop or vegetation shadows are outside this slice.

## Cascade fit and sampling

- Use two 1024 × 1024 depth maps with fixed receiver depth bands of 0–24 m and 24–64 m, clipped by the camera near and far planes. Fit both maps across a 22–26 m overlap and blend visibility using smoothstep across it. Fade visibility toward fully lit over 60–64 m. Depth is positive view-space metres as in E. Keep map size, split and fade fixed through the initial qualification.
- Build each orthographic projection from the canonical camera slice with a stable sun basis. Use a bounding sphere for XY extent so changing camera orientation does not change the extent at fixed lens, aspect and split. Include a fixed texel guard band for snapping and filtering. Quantize light-space XY centre on the canonical texel grid before forming the local matrix, using double precision through the subtraction of the local origin. Record the snap rounding rule and test negative coordinates, grid ties and a sun near the basis fallback. Bounds must include the receiver slice after snapping.
- Select casters from the immutable resident batches of the installed generation using conservative intersections with each light volume. Extrude the receiver footprint toward the sun through the eligible resident bounds and include batches outside the camera frustum that can shadow a receiver. Fit depth to receivers and selected casters with finite padding. Exclude private candidates, retired batches and geometry outside admitted residency. Use deterministic batch order with bounded reusable scratch and no extra brick requests.
- Sample depth with a fixed bounded comparison filter and defined border behavior. Specify depth compare, constant and slope bias in the renderer PR. Bias must preserve contact on the fixed rock fixture and avoid self-shadowing at low sun. Zero direct light can skip both shadow passes. Receivers outside the two bands retain directional illumination and ambient with full visibility.

## Publication and GPU lifetime

Record both shadow depth passes and the terrain color pass against the same installed mesh generation in one submission. Reuse the existing batch vertex and index buffers. A successful Present identifying a new mesh generation must contain that generation's shadows. A candidate that is stale, rejected or held never contributes to an earlier generation's shadows. Camera and sun changes can redraw the maps using resident geometry. No mesh repacking, upload, terrain generation advance or collision mutation is caused by such updates.

Transition maps from depth-write to shader-resource state before sampling and preserve ordering when the same maps return to depth-write. Keep map dimensions independent of the presentation surface. Account for both maps and any overlapping resources that remain in custody during replacement or teardown. Release them after proven queue retirement or confirmed device removal under existing Groundwork ownership rules. Preserve Package G's exclusive near/far owner handoff and retained session queue. The far renderer keeps its existing presentation.

## WYU integration

Use the shared Package E integer tick and presentation fraction to derive the canonical sun. When G is on, use that same value for sky disk, direct light and shadow projection. Define the Far Horizon direct and ambient curve and fixed test poses in the WYU integration PR with midday, low sun and night cases. Continue derives sunlight from the saved tick and pose with the existing checkpoint. Ember Caves retains its existing rendering. Pin the verified immutable archive from green Groundwork master. B and E stay active in both WP15 performance variants and G stays in the same state per pair.

## Acceptance

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| WARP and NVIDIA image tests | Require WARP readback in Windows CI and exercise NVIDIA with Debug Layer and DRED. A fixed rock and receiver verify expected lit and occluded points for two sun directions, low-sun normal response, and E fog on both surfaces. Include an off-frustum resident caster, seams, sky enabled, disabled fallback, and invalid frame rejection without consuming a sequence. |
| Cascade stability | Compare matched canonical camera and sun before and after rebase. Map a selected edge into the affected cascade and require displacement ≤1 texel. Record camera samples across overlap and fade during slow movement and quick turns. Check that transition samples use the overlap weights and do not abruptly drop a valid caster at the split. |
| Edit and lifecycle | Show the changed shadow on the first successfully presented frame of the edited mesh generation, using both sequence and generation evidence plus pixel samples. Verify held, rejected and stale work, resize, stop, GPU settlement and device-loss custody. Confirm no extra upload or brick request for light/camera-only frames. |
| WYU route | Fixed midday, low-sun, overlap crossing, quick turn, edit and rebase on Far Horizon. Check Package G disk alignment when enabled, local fog, Continue and clean shutdown. Verify exact package provenance and retain outputs and fixture parameters. |
| GPU time | Windows x64 Release at 1920 × 1080 on the WP11 reference class. Same build, camera/route, seed, presentation mode, driver and B/E/G settings within each off/on pair. At least five independent runs and 2,000 frames per variant per phase after warm-up. Report midday, low sun and quick turns separately with raw timestamps and per-run and pooled percentiles. Separate the shadow passes, incremental terrain shading and total GPU frame time. Shadow passes plus incremental terrain shading must meet p95 ≤1.5 ms and p99 ≤2.0 ms, and total GPU frame deltas must meet +10% p95 and +15% p99 per phase. |
| CPU, memory and work | Cascade setup, caster selection and pass recording together p99 ≤0.30 ms with zero shadow-related heap allocations per frame after warm-up. Report ≤16 MiB incremental peak GPU memory for shadow resources including retained copies, separately from ≤4 extra GPU bytes per resident near-terrain vertex. At most two depth passes and one extra draw per admitted batch per cascade. Measure native draws and uploads and streaming requests. Sun or camera updates add zero geometry uploads and shadows add zero brick requests. |
| Production | Repeat with WP11 reference limits and pressure cases, including full speculative CPU queue and blocked speculative GPU streaming while protected movement and edits progress. Report holds, stale results, and retirement. Workstation evidence is tracked separately.
