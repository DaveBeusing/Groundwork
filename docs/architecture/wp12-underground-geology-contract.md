# WP12 · Underground geology and caves

**Status** Proposed for review. Implementation starts after contract acceptance.

## Outcome and baseline

A WYU player can select the WP12 generator, find a naturally generated entrance from the surface, descend through visibly distinct rock strata, expose and extract an ore deposit, then save underground and resume or return to the surface. An independently generated sealed chamber and an overhang exercise terrain that cannot be represented as a height field. The route must work with ordinary game controls and the same terrain that rendering, movement and editing query.

The technical baseline is [WP11](wp11-procedural-planet-contract.md) and its [workstation handover](../wp11-handover.md) on Groundwork master at `c17469b747a259859dcef8089511a57283a7b263`. Keep the planet-centred Int64 lattice, canonical vertices and owned material cells, sparse bricks, Marching Cubes/Transvoxel, terrain publication boundary, GPU retirement and NTFS checkpoint recovery. Cube-sphere charts and render origins remain view coordinates. WP11 Q02 [#89](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/issues/89), Q01 [#88](https://github.com/DaveBeusing/Groundwork/issues/88) and WP11 production acceptance remain open for reference qualification. WP12 contract review can proceed on the verified baseline. Any WP11 defect that invalidates a WP12 gate must be tracked with its failing case before WP12 claims that gate.

## G1 · Versioned 3D geology and cave generation

- Groundwork owns a portable generator and validated recipe. WP12 initially uses generator version 2 in the WP11 planet family. The canonical recipe digest covers the cave layout, depth envelope, rock and ore palette and thresholds, and all sampling parameters. Freeze recipe encoding, numeric bounds, rounding, stream IDs, algorithm and golden vectors in the generator PR before publishing a consumable package. Version 1 keeps its existing recipe and sample outputs.
- Use integer or fixed-point sampling on canonical lattice coordinates. Key strata, cave topology and ores on separate, stable seed streams. Rock layers follow depth under the uncarved landform with bounded 3D perturbation. Define depth from the WP11 base density, using the same quantization as the surface. Ore is a deterministic cell material within eligible host rock. Palette IDs are stable within the schema, and geometry, collision and mine eligibility depend on committed density and the resolved material at the same revision. Empty samples grant no ore.
- Use deterministic bounded spatial feature cells for tunnels, chambers and entrance shafts. Stable feature keys are derived from the seed and signed global feature-cell coordinates. Adjacent cells agree on shared portals through a canonical pair key and an order-independent portal position. Curves and chambers have bounded influence and are discoverable by a fixed neighbourhood derived from that influence. Sampling work has a recipe-defined upper bound and uses no global connectivity scan or generation-order state. Validate all scales, depth bounds and arithmetic before work admission. Reject inputs outside the supported radius or coordinate range.
- For negative-solid base density B and a signed cavity field C whose interior is positive and whose boundary is zero, natural density is max(B, C). Outside the cavity's influence and approved depth envelope, density equals B. Natural excavation can only remove solid. Surface cuts are restricted to designated entrances. For the controlled route, seed selection and initial spawn must locate the entrance using a bounded deterministic search with stable tie-breaking. A world with no valid route spawn returns a visible failure. A chamber fixture must retain a solid roof, and a passage fixture must maintain clearance for the declared player capsule at the collision LOD. Define minimum passage and shell thickness against that resolution. Coarse far meshes may simplify underground detail where they cannot be used for movement or target selection.
- Apply committed WP11 edit overlays after natural generation. Adds and removals use the existing atomic publication path and canonical edits across rock, ore and cave surfaces. Ore extraction must be tied to a committed removal from an eligible solid cell. WYU owns yield, inventory and UI. Duplicate input, stale revision, rejected edits and retry after recovery must not award duplicate yield. The same committed gameplay save cut contains edits and the associated inventory change.

## G2 · Streaming, collision and presentation

- Drive underground interest from 3D player location, swept movement corridor and active edit footprint. Radial surface distance alone cannot evict traversable underground coverage. Use local collision detail and existing mixed-LOD transitions for entrances and nearby tunnels. Far terrain remains render-only. Preserve radial gravity and support normals from the same committed density. Rebase updates camera and terrain coherently and leaves canonical keys intact.
- Admit generation, meshing, collision and uploads against explicit caps for queued jobs, brick leases, candidate geometry, active geometry and GPU-retained generations. The admission footprint includes edit neighbours, seams, collision stencils and held old generations. The qualification PR records actual cap values, peak counts/bytes and latency percentiles per phase. Treat saturation as bounded backpressure. Maintain protected progress with speculative capacity full and uploads blocked. On insufficient protected capacity, keep the old publication and hold movement at the last valid pose or reject the edit explicitly.
- Treat unknown, delayed, failed or evicted density as unavailable coverage. Do not synthesize walkable air or move into an unvalidated region. Reject stale completions on world, terrain and view identity. Preserve the last coherent publication during cancellation or failure. Release CPU leases after their users finish and retire GPU objects only under existing fence or confirmed device-loss rules. Expose diagnostics for stalls, quota rejection, obsolete work and teardown.

## G3 · Save, compatibility and recovery

- Dispatch through the saved generator and recipe identity. A new WP12 world selects version 2 explicitly. Version 1 worlds reopen with version 1 and unchanged generation and overlays. Never regenerate a version 1 world as version 2. Reuse the WP11 volume checkpoint envelope if its existing fields and validation can represent the new recipe and the bounded WYU state. A format change requires its own schema version and explicit dispatch.
- Restore recipe and version, canonical density and material overlays, player pose and game state from one committed checkpoint. Derived geology, meshes and GPU state are rebuilt. Validate capsule clearance and support at the saved pose before releasing movement. A failed restore preserves the save and surfaces a recoverable error. Unsupported generator, unknown recipe and incompatible or damaged data must retain their bytes and must not silently initialize a new world over an existing slot.
- Use WP11's NTFS transaction and recovery rules. Verify interruption before install, after one member is replaced and after full commit. Recovery yields a complete previous or complete new generation, with matching terrain, material, ore inventory and pose. The save in a held terrain transition captures the currently published state.

Groundwork owns geometry, generation and portable runtime semantics. WYU owns the route, light, palette presentation, inventory and game progression. WP12 covers static generated strata and deposits with player-driven changes. Simulated tectonics, fluids, cave ecosystems, planetary biomes and vegetation, construction and multiplayer are later scope.
